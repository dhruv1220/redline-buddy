# Reseller Agreement — Northwind Technologies Channel Partner Program

**Vendor:** Northwind Technologies, Inc. ("Vendor")
**Reseller:** Contoso Systems LLC ("Reseller")
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2026

## 1. Appointment

Vendor appoints Reseller as a reseller of the Products in the Territory.

## 2. Exclusivity

Reseller's appointment is exclusive with respect to competing products: Reseller shall not market or sell any competing products during the term of this Agreement or for twelve (12) months thereafter.

## 3. Pricing

Vendor may modify pricing, discounts, or program terms on thirty (30) days' notice posted to the partner portal; Reseller's continued selling after such notice constitutes acceptance of the modified terms. Vendor may increase prices at any time without cap.

## 4. Quotas

Reseller commits to a minimum revenue quota of $500,000 per quarter. Failure to meet quota in any two consecutive quarters is grounds for immediate termination.

## 5. Termination

Vendor may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 6. Direct Sales

Vendor reserves the right to sell Products directly to any customer, including customers introduced by Reseller, at any price.

## 7. Indemnification

Reseller shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Vendor against any third-party claims of intellectual property infringement arising from the Products.

## 8. Term

This Agreement has an initial term of two (2) years.
