# SELF-STORAGE RENTAL AGREEMENT — CLEAN SAMPLE

SecureStore Facilities ("Operator") — Rental Agreement for Renter, Unit B-214.

## Rent

Monthly rent: $180. Rent increases are capped at 10% per 12-month
period and require 30 days' written notice.

## Late payment and lien

Rent is due on the 1st. Late fees are limited to 5% of monthly rent
per occurrence. No lockout occurs until rent is at least 10 days past
due and written notice has been sent. No lien sale occurs until rent
is at least 90 days past due, after certified-mail notice at 60 days.

## Liability

Operator remains liable for loss caused by its negligence, including
failure to maintain gates, lighting, and roofing.

## Insurance

Renter may use any policy with at least $2,000 of coverage; the
facility's insurance is optional.

## Access

Renter has coded gate access 24 hours per day, 7 days per week.
