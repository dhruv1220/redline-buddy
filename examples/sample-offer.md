# Offer of Employment — Sample (test fixture)

Dear Candidate,

On behalf of Acme Robotics, Inc. (the "Company"), we are pleased to offer you
the position of Senior AI Engineer, reporting to the VP of Engineering. Your
anticipated start date is November 2, 2026, contingent on successful completion
of a background check.

## Compensation

You will receive a competitive compensation package commensurate with your
experience, paid semi-monthly, subject to standard payroll taxes and
withholdings. Details of the Company's benefits programs will be provided
under separate cover.

## Employment Relationship

Your employment with the Company is "at-will," meaning that either you or the
Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without
cause or notice.

## Restrictive Covenants

During your employment and for twelve (12) months after termination, you shall
not engage in or work for any competing business in the robotics or artificial
intelligence industries.

## Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising out of this offer or your employment shall be resolved
exclusively through binding arbitration administered by JAMS, and you waive
any right to participate in a class action waiver proceeding against the
Company.

We are excited about the possibility of you joining the team. Please sign and
return this letter by October 15, 2026 to accept.

Sincerely,
Jane Smith, Head of People
Acme Robotics, Inc.
