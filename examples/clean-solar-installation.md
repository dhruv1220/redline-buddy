# RESIDENTIAL SOLAR PPA — SAMPLE (homeowner-favorable / clean)

This Solar Power Purchase Agreement is between ClearSky Solar Inc.
("Installer") and the Homeowner for the property at 5678 Oak Avenue.

## 1. Term and rate

The initial term is twenty-five (25) years at a fixed $0.16/kWh with
no annual escalator for the full 25-year term. The rate never changes.

## 2. Ownership and title

Homeowner owns the System outright after installation. Installer
provides a lien waiver upon final payment and files nothing against
the Property title.

## 3. Roof warranty

Installer warrants all roof penetrations against leaks and water
intrusion for ten (10) years, and is liable for any roof damage
caused by the installation, repaired at Installer's sole cost.

## 4. Downtime credits

If the System is offline for more than fifteen (15) consecutive days
for reasons other than Homeowner's fault, payments abate pro rata for
each day offline.

## 5. Removal and reinstallation

Installer will remove and reinstall the System at no additional cost
to Homeowner whenever roof maintenance, repair, or replacement
requires it.

## 6. Sale of home

Transfer of this Agreement to a home buyer will not be unreasonably
withheld or delayed beyond fifteen (15) business days. The transfer
fee is capped at $250, and Installer provides a written buyout
schedule on request.

## 7. Production commitment

Installer guarantees minimum annual production of 9,800 kWh for the
25-year term. Shortfalls below 90% of estimated output are credited
against payments at the PPA rate.

## 8. Written promises

All material sales representations, including estimated savings and
production figures, are attached as Exhibit D and incorporated into
this Agreement.
