# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT — Round 2 (vendor's revised draft)

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026,
by and between Acme Cloud Inc. ("Vendor") and Customer.

## 1. Services

Vendor shall provide the subscription services described in each Order Form.

## 2. Term

The initial term of this Agreement is twelve (12) months from the Effective Date.
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms.

## 3. Fees and Payment

Customer shall pay all fees set forth in the applicable Order Form within fifteen
(15) days of invoice.

## 4. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach on thirty (30) days' written
notice if the breach remains uncured. Termination for any other reason is not
permitted during the initial term.

## 5. Indemnification

Each party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other against
third-party claims. Customer shall indemnify Vendor for claims arising from
Customer's use of the Services, and Vendor shall indemnify Customer for claims
alleging that the Services infringe a third party's intellectual property rights.

## 6. Limitation of Liability

Each party's aggregate liability shall be subject to a limitation of liability
equal to the fees paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to keep the other's Confidential Information confidential and
to use it only to perform this Agreement.
