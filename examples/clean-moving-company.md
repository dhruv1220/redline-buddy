# HOUSEHOLD MOVING CONTRACT — SAMPLE (consumer-favorable / clean)

This Moving Services Agreement is between Careful Carrier Inc.
("Mover," USDOT 1234567) and the Customer for a move from San Jose,
CA to Austin, TX.

## 1. Estimate

This is a binding not-to-exceed estimate of $4,200. Final charges
will not exceed the estimate unless Customer signs a written change
order for additional services.

## 2. Valuation

Full value protection is included at no extra charge: lost or
damaged articles are repaired or replaced at current replacement
cost.

## 3. Deposit

A refundable booking deposit of $200 holds your moving date, credited
in full toward the final charges.

## 4. Payment and delivery

Payment is due when your goods are delivered and unloaded. Any
disputed charges are documented in writing and resolved after
delivery.

## 5. Delivery schedule

Mover guarantees delivery by June 14, 2026. Delays beyond the
guaranteed date are credited at $150 per day.

## 6. Carrier

Mover is the licensed motor carrier performing this move (USDOT
1234567). Your goods are never brokered to a third party.

## 7. Claims

You have 9 months from delivery to file a written claim for loss or
damage. Claims are acknowledged within 30 days of receipt.

## 8. Pricing

The price above is all-inclusive: stairs, long carries, fuel,
shuttle service, and standard packing materials are included in the
quoted price.
