# CHILDCARE ENROLLMENT AGREEMENT — SAMPLE (parent-unfavorable)

Sunshine Daycare Center ("Center") — Enrollment Agreement for the Child.

## Enrollment

A registration fee of $350 is due at signing and is non-refundable.
The enrollment deposit of $500 is non-refundable under all circumstances,
even if the Child never attends.

## Tuition

Monthly tuition: $2,200. Full tuition is due for all absences, vacations,
and illness — no credit, refund, or discount applies when the Child is
absent. Full tuition is also due during Center holidays and staff
training days; no make-up days are offered.

## Withdrawal

Withdrawal requires 60 days' written notice. Full tuition is billed
through the entire 60-day notice period regardless of attendance.

## Rate changes

The Center reserves the right to increase tuition at any time. Rate
increases may apply without prior notice.

## Late pick-up

Late pick-up is billed at $3 per minute from the scheduled pick-up time,
with no grace period.

## Liability

Parent waives all liability and claims against the Center for any injury
to the Child, including injury caused by the Center's negligence, and
the Center is not responsible for any harm occurring on the premises.

## Photos

Parent consents to the Center photographing the Child and using photos
in marketing materials and social media posts.
