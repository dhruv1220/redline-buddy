# CLEAN — Convertible Note (founder-friendly draft, fires 0 convertible-note rules)

CONVERTIBLE PROMISSORY NOTE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Convertible Promissory Note Purchase Agreement is entered into as
of March 15, 2026, by and among Acme Robotics, Inc. (the "Company")
and the purchasers listed on Exhibit A.

1. Issuance of Notes. The Company shall issue convertible promissory
notes in the aggregate principal amount of $750,000.

2. Maturity. The Notes mature twenty-four (24) months after issuance
(the "Maturity Date"). If no Qualified Financing has occurred by the
Maturity Date, the Notes automatically convert into Common Stock at
the Valuation Cap; the Notes are never repayable in cash.

3. Interest. The Notes bear no interest.

4. Change of Control. Upon a Change of Control, each Note converts
into Common Stock at the Valuation Cap (a 1x payout with no premium
and no multiple).

5. Pro-Rata Rights. Each Noteholder who invested at least $25,000 has
pro-rata rights in the next equity financing, based on as-converted
ownership.

6. Conversion Price. The Notes convert at a 20% discount to the
Qualified Financing price, subject to a $3,000,000 post-money valuation
cap, applying whichever is more favorable to the Company.

7. Qualified Financing. "Qualified Financing" means an equity financing
with aggregate gross proceeds of at least $1 million.

8. Noteholder Consent. The Notes may be amended only with the consent
of a majority of the Noteholders; the Noteholders have no other
consent or veto rights.

9. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
State of Delaware.
