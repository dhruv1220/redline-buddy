# SAMPLE IP ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT, BALANCED — Novacore Systems

## 1. Assignment and Consideration
In consideration of $5,000 paid to Assignor, Assignor assigns to Novacore
Systems the Inventions conceived within the scope of Assignor's work for the
Company, relating to the Company's business, or developed using Company
resources.

## 2. Prior Inventions
The assignment excludes the Prior Inventions listed on Exhibit A, which
remain the sole property of Assignor.

## 3. Term
The assignment and disclosure obligations end on Assignor's last day of
service. Inventions conceived after that date are not assigned.

## 4. Disclosure
Assignor shall disclose Inventions within the scope of this assignment and
execute reasonable documents to perfect the Company's rights, at the
Company's expense.

## 5. License-Back
The Company grants Assignor a perpetual, royalty-free license-back to use the
assigned Inventions outside the Company's field of business.

## 6. Moral Rights and Open Source
Assignor retains moral rights of attribution on published works. Open-source
contributions are excluded from this assignment when made on Assignor's own time.
