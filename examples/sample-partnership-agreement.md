# SAMPLE PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT — Harborline Ventures (unfavorable to the Partners)

## 1. Formation and Ownership
Alex Rivera and Jordan Kim form Harborline Ventures, a general partnership.
Each Partner holds a 50% ownership interest, complete and unconditional from
the signing date, with no forfeiture provisions of any kind.

## 2. Management
All Major Decisions of the Partnership require the unanimous consent of both
Partners. There is no mechanism for resolving disagreements: if the Partners
cannot agree, the Partnership simply takes no action until they do.

## 3. Capital
Each Partner's Initial Contribution is $75,000. The Partners shall make such
additional capital contributions as the Partnership requires from time to time.
A Partner who fails to fund a required contribution within 15 days shall have
their ownership interest diluted in proportion to the shortfall.

## 4. Partner Exit
If a Partner dies, becomes disabled, or voluntarily withdraws, their ownership
interest passes to their heirs or remains with the departing Partner. The
Partnership has no obligation and no right to acquire the interest, and the
remaining Partner must continue the business with the successor.

## 5. Intellectual Property
Each Partner retains all right, title, and interest in their respective
intellectual property, including anything developed before or during the
Partnership. Nothing in this Agreement transfers any intellectual property to
the Partnership.

## 6. Distributions
Distributions are made only when both Partners unanimously agree. The
Partnership may retain all profits in the business indefinitely, and no
Partner is entitled to any distribution to cover taxes on allocated income.

## 7. Duties Between Partners
To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Partners waive any fiduciary
duties of loyalty or care to one another, and each Partner may pursue outside
business opportunities without disclosure.

## 8. Post-Departure Restrictions
A Partner who leaves the Partnership for any reason shall not compete with
the Partnership's business for three (3) years following departure, anywhere
in the United States, and signs a covenant not to compete covering the
entire nationwide market.
