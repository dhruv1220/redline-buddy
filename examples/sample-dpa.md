# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into as of August 1, 2026,
by and between Acme Cloud Inc. ("Processor") and Customer ("Controller").

## 1. Scope

Processor shall process personal data on behalf of Controller as necessary
to provide the subscription services.

## 2. Instructions

Processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from
Controller.

## 3. Confidentiality

Processor shall ensure that persons authorized to process personal data have
committed themselves to confidentiality.

## 4. Subprocessors

Processor may engage subprocessors to assist in providing the services.
A current list of subprocessors is available on Processor's website.

## 5. Assistance

Processor shall assist Controller in responding to data subject requests.

## 6. Term

This DPA remains in effect for as long as Processor processes personal data
on behalf of Controller.
