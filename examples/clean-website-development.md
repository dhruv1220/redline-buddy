# SAMPLE WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT, BALANCED — PixelForge Studio

## 1. Scope and Ownership
PixelForge will design and build the Client's e-commerce website for $12,000.
Upon final payment, PixelForge assigns the Deliverables to Client, including
all source code and design files.

## 2. Delivery and Acceptance
PixelForge delivers complete source code, build scripts, and documentation
with each milestone. The Client has a 15-day acceptance testing period per
milestone; PixelForge shall cure defects within 15 days, and the final
milestone is due upon written acceptance.

## 3. Schedule
Beta delivery date: May 1. Launch date: June 1. Milestone dates are listed in
Exhibit A; delays beyond 7 days accrue a 5% per-week credit against fees.

## 4. Warranty
PixelForge provides a 90-day warranty period after acceptance, fixing defects
at no additional charge.

## 5. Changes
Change Orders require the Client's written approval of a fixed fee before
work begins; hourly change work is capped at $1,000 per month.

## 6. Third-Party Components
PixelForge lists all third-party plugins and themes in Exhibit B, transfers
all licenses to Client, and discloses recurring license fees before
acceptance.

## 7. Disputes
Fee disputes go to mediation while the site remains live; neither party may
interrupt service over a billing disagreement.
