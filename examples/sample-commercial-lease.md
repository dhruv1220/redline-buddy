# COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is entered into as of June 1, 2026, between Parkside Retail LLC
("Landlord") and Brightline Coffee Co. ("Tenant") for Suite 120 at Parkside
Plaza.

## 1. Term

The initial term is five (5) years.

## 2. Rent

Base rent is $8,000 per month. Tenant shall also pay its pro rata share of
all common area maintenance and operating expenses for the center as a
pass-through, reconciled annually.

## 3. Exclusive Use

Tenant is granted the exclusive right to operate a coffee shop in the
center; Landlord shall not lease to any other coffee or beverage business.

## 4. Assignment

Tenant may assign this lease or sublet the premises at any time.

## 5. Casualty

If the premises are damaged by casualty, Tenant may elect to terminate;
the lease otherwise continues at reduced rent during restoration.

## 6. Holdover

If Tenant holds over after expiration, the tenancy continues month to month
at the base rent then in effect.

## 7. Relocation

Landlord may relocate Tenant to any other space in the center upon
ninety (90) days' notice.

## 8. Tenant Entity

Tenant is a Delaware limited liability company. The lease is signed by the
company only.

## 9. Insurance

Each party shall carry property insurance on its own property.
