# HOME IMPROVEMENT CONTRACT

This Home Improvement Contract ("Contract") is between Apex Renovations LLC
("Contractor") and the undersigned homeowner ("Owner"), dated May 4, 2026.

## 1. The Work

Contractor agrees to remodel Owner's kitchen, including new cabinets,
countertops, flooring, and appliances.

## 2. Contract Price and Deposit

The total contract price is $85,000. Owner shall pay a deposit of 50% of the
contract price upon signing this Contract. The balance is payable in monthly
installments as invoiced by Contractor.

## 3. Pricing Basis

The contract price is estimated on a time-and-materials basis at Contractor's
standard hourly rates, plus the cost of materials with a 20% markup. The final
amount may exceed the estimate depending on actual hours worked.

## 4. Schedule

Work will begin promptly after permits are issued and will proceed diligently
until finished.

## 5. Permits

Owner is responsible for obtaining all required building permits and scheduling
inspections.

## 6. Extra Work

Any extra work requested by Owner during the project will be billed at
Contractor's standard rates and added to the next invoice.

## 7. Final Payment

The final payment of 10% is due before final inspection, upon Contractor's
invoice.

## 8. Assignment

Contractor may assign or subcontract any portion of the Work without Owner's
consent.

## 9. Disputes

All disputes arising under this Contract shall be resolved by binding
arbitration in accordance with the rules of the American Arbitration
Association. The arbitrator's decision shall be final.
