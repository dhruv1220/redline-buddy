# Employment Agreement — bare-bones draft (employer review)

This Employment Agreement is between Startup Inc. ("Company") and
Jordan Lee ("Employee"), effective January 5, 2026.

## Role and Compensation

Employee is hired as Senior Engineer at a base salary of $160,000 per
year. Employee waives any right to overtime compensation.

## Non-Competition

Employee agrees not to compete with the Company for 24 months after
termination of employment, within the State of California.

## Severance

Employee will receive three months' severance upon termination without
cause.

## Term

This agreement begins on the effective date and continues until
terminated by either party.

## General

This Agreement is the entire understanding between the parties.
