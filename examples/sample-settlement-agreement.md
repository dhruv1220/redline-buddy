# Settlement Agreement and Release

This Settlement Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 3, 2026
(the "Effective Date"), by and between Acme Corp ("Company") and Jordan Lee
("Claimant").

## 1. Settlement Payment

The Company shall pay Claimant the sum of $45,000 (the "Settlement Amount")
as soon as practicable after the Effective Date.

## 2. Dismissal

Claimant shall file the dismissal with prejudice within ten (10) days of
execution, and the Company shall pay promptly thereafter.

## 3. Release

Claimant hereby grants the Company a general release of all claims, known or
unknown, arising from any matter through the Effective Date, including an
express waiver of California Civil Code Section 1542.

## 4. Non-Disparagement

Claimant shall not disparage the Company, its officers, directors, or
products, in any public or private forum.

## 5. Confidentiality

Claimant shall keep the settlement terms confidential. This confidentiality
obligation binds Claimant alone and survives termination of this Agreement.

## 6. Entire Agreement

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties regarding the
dispute, and supersedes all prior discussions.
