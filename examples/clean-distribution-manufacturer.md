# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT — SAMPLE (balanced)

This Distribution Agreement is between Vertex Devices Inc.
("Manufacturer") and Global Retail Partners LLC ("Distributor").

## 1. Appointment — exclusive with minimums

Manufacturer appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor for
the Products in the Territory, conditioned on Distributor meeting the
minimum annual purchase commitments in Exhibit B. If Distributor
fails to meet them in any year, the appointment becomes non-exclusive.

## 2. Trademarks

Distributor's use of Manufacturer's trademarks is subject to
Manufacturer's brand guidelines, and Distributor shall obtain
Manufacturer's prior written approval for all marketing materials
bearing the marks.

## 3. Local adaptations

All localizations, adaptations, and derivative works of the Products
are owned exclusively by Manufacturer. Distributor receives a
non-exclusive license to use them during the term only.

## 4. Post-term

No non-competition covenant applies to Manufacturer after expiration
or termination. Confidentiality and IP provisions survive.

## 5. Termination

Either party may terminate for cause on sixty (60) days' written
notice and opportunity to cure. Distributor may terminate for
convenience on twelve months' written notice, and shall pay a
termination fee equal to Manufacturer's committed but unrecovered
capacity costs.

## 6. Pricing

Wholesale prices are set out in Exhibit C. Price-matching applies
only to purchases of comparable annual volume tiers, and never below
Manufacturer's published distributor floor price.

## 7. Sales reporting and audit

Distributor shall provide quarterly sales reports. Manufacturer may
audit Distributor's sales records once per year on thirty (30) days'
notice; if underreporting exceeds five percent (5%), Distributor
bears the audit cost.

## 8. Distributor obligations

Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote and
sell the Products, submit an annual business plan, and meet quarterly
with Manufacturer for performance review.
