## 1. Confidentiality Obligations

Recipient shall keep the Discloser's confidential information confidential.
Confidential Information excludes information that is publicly available,
independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party.

## 2. Survival

These obligations survive for three (3) years from disclosure.

## 3. Return of Materials

On termination, Recipient shall return or destroy all confidential materials
and certify destruction in writing.
