# INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
June 15, 2026, by and between Northwind Labs Inc. ("Company") and Jane Doe
("Contractor").

## 1. Services

Contractor shall provide software development services as described in
Exhibit A, working approximately 30 hours per week.

## 2. Compensation

Contractor shall be paid $150 per hour. Contractor shall submit invoices
monthly, and Company shall pay each invoice within 45 days of receipt.

## 3. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the project
is complete. Either party may terminate this Agreement only for material
breach that remains uncured 30 days after written notice.

## 4. Restrictive Covenants

During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months thereafter,
Contractor shall not engage in or perform services for any business that
competes with the Company.

## 5. Expenses

Contractor shall be reimbursed for all reasonable business expenses incurred
in connection with the Services.

## 6. Relationship of Parties

Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Company.
