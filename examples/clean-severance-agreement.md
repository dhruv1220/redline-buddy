# SEVERANCE AGREEMENT (sample — balanced, employee-fair)

This Severance Agreement is entered into by and between TechCorp Inc.
("Company") and Employee.

## 1. Separation

Employee's employment ends effective March 15, 2026. Employee will receive
twelve (12) weeks of severance pay, subject to standard payroll withholding.
Employee is advised to consult a tax advisor regarding timing.

## 2. Release

This release excludes vested retirement benefits, workers' compensation
claims, unemployment insurance rights, and any claims that cannot be
released as a matter of law.

## 3. Non-Disparagement

Non-disparagement is mutual: neither party shall disparage the other, its
officers, or its products.

## 4. Cooperation

Employee's cooperation in any Company matter is limited to reasonable
availability on fourteen days' notice, compensated at $250 per hour plus
expenses.

## 5. Competitive Restrictions

No post-employment competitive restrictions apply. Any prior competitive
restriction terminates on the separation date.

## 6. Future Employment

Employee remains eligible for future roles with the Company after twelve
(12) months from the separation date.

## 7. Health Coverage

The Company shall pay Employee's COBRA premiums for six (6) months
following the separation date.

## 8. Consideration Period

You have twenty-one (21) days to consider this agreement and seven (7)
days after signing to revoke it in writing.
