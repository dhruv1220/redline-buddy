# Settlement Agreement and Release (Claimant-Friendly)

This Settlement Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 3, 2026
(the "Effective Date"), by and between Acme Corp ("Company") and Jordan Lee
("Claimant").

## 1. Settlement Payment

The Company shall pay Claimant $45,000 (the "Settlement Amount") within 10
business days of the Effective Date.

## 2. Release

Claimant releases all known claims arising from the Dispute described in
Section 5. The release is limited to claims known to Claimant as of the
Effective Date and does not waive any protection Claimant has under
applicable law for claims that cannot be released.

## 3. Dismissal

After the settlement payment clears Claimant's account, Claimant shall file
the dismissal with prejudice.

## 4. Non-Disparagement

Neither party shall disparage the other. This is a mutual non-disparagement
obligation, and a breaching party pays liquidated damages of $5,000 per
occurrence.

## 5. Confidentiality

Both parties shall keep the settlement terms confidential, except for
disclosures to legal counsel, tax advisors, or as required by law.

## 6. Tax Treatment

The Settlement Amount will be reported on Form 1099-MISC as other income,
with no withholding. Each party is responsible for its own taxes arising
from the Settlement Amount.

## 7. Late Payment

Amounts unpaid when due bear interest at 1.5% per month from the due date
until paid.

## 8. Enforcement

The prevailing party in any action to enforce this Agreement recovers
reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.
