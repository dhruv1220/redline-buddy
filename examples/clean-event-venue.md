# Venue Rental Agreement — Balanced Terms

**Venue:** Grand Ballroom Events Center ("Venue")
**Organizer:** Brightline Conferences LLC ("Organizer")
**Event Date:** June 12, 2027

## 1. Rental

The rental period includes four (4) hours of setup and four (4) hours of teardown at no additional charge.

## 2. Food & Beverage

The F&B minimum carries a twenty-five percent (25%) attrition allowance; the final guarantee is due seventy-two (72) hours before the event.

## 3. Cancellation

Cancellation twelve (12) or more months out forfeits only the deposit; the sliding scale applies inside six (6) months.

## 4. Vendors

Organizer may use outside vendors providing certificates of insurance; in-house vendor pricing is capped at market rates.

## 5. Damage

Damage liability is capped and excludes reasonable wear and tear; condition is documented in a joint walkthrough before and after the event.

## 6. Date Certainty

Venue shall not relocate or reschedule the event without Organizer's written consent; venue-initiated cancellation refunds all fees plus documented rebooking costs.

## 7. Sound

Amplified sound is permitted until 11:00 PM at up to 95 dB, as confirmed for the event program.

## 8. Force Majeure

Force majeure includes pandemic, epidemic, government orders, natural disaster, and performer illness; Organizer may reschedule once without fee before cancellation terms apply.
