# SAMPLE HOME IMPROVEMENT CONTRACT
(Deliberate red flags below — fixture for the home-improvement playbook.)

1. Scope

Contractor shall remodel the kitchen, including cabinets, countertops, and
flooring, for a fixed price of $45,000.

2. Payment

Homeowner shall pay a 50% deposit upon signing. The remaining 50% is due when
Contractor says the work is finished.

3. Schedule

Contractor will start when materials arrive and will work steadily until done.
