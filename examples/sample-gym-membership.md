# GYM MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT — SAMPLE (member-unfavorable)

IronWorks Fitness ("Club") — Membership Agreement for Member.

## Term and renewal

Initial term: 12 months. Membership will automatically renew for
successive 12-month terms (evergreen renewal) unless Member provides
written notice 60 days before term end. No renewal reminder is sent.

## Dues and fees

Monthly dues: $49. An annual maintenance fee of $59 is charged each
January. Initiation fee of $199, non-refundable, due at signing.

## Cancellation

Member must cancel in person at the home club or by certified mail.
Cancellation is effective 30 days after the Club processes the notice;
dues continue during the notice period. Phone, email, and online
cancellation are not accepted.

## Freeze policy

Membership freeze is not permitted except for documented medical
reasons, limited to 30 days per year, subject to a $25 freeze fee.

## Personal training

Training packages: 10 sessions, $700. Sessions expire 60 days from
purchase; unused sessions are non-refundable. Training packages
auto-renew for another 10 sessions unless cancelled in writing.

## Liability

Member waives all liability of the Club for any injury, including
injury caused by the Club's negligence, and assumes all risk of using
the facilities.

## Closures

Dues continue during any closure for renovation or maintenance. No
refund or credit is given for closure periods.
