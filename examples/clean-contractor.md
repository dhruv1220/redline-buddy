# Independent Contractor Agreement — Clean (test fixture: fires nothing on contractor)

## 1. Services and Compensation

Contractor will provide software development services at $150 per hour,
invoicing monthly. Client shall pay each invoice within 15 days (net 15).

## 2. Intellectual Property

Contractor hereby assigns to the Company all intellectual property in the
work product created under this agreement.

## 3. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement at will on fourteen (14) days'
written notice.

## 4. Confidentiality

Contractor shall not disclose the Company's confidential information, during
or for three years after the engagement.

## 5. Expenses

Reasonable expenses are reimbursed with pre-approval and itemized receipts.
