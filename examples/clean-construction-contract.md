# HOME IMPROVEMENT CONTRACT

This Home Improvement Contract ("Contract") is between Harbor & Pine Builders
Inc. ("Contractor") and the undersigned homeowner ("Owner"), dated June 15, 2026.

## 1. Scope of Work

The scope of work is fully described in Exhibit A, including the itemized list
of tasks, materials, finishes, and the plans and specifications dated June 1,
2026, which are incorporated by reference.

## 2. Contract Price and Payments

The fixed contract price is $85,000. Owner shall pay a deposit of 10% of the
contract price upon signing. Further payments follow the milestone schedule in
Exhibit B: 40% after rough-in, 30% after cabinets and countertops are set, and
the final 20% within 10 days after final inspection and Owner's written
acceptance of the completed Work, including all punch-list items.

## 3. Schedule

Work shall commence on the start date of July 7, 2026 and reach substantial
completion no later than October 30, 2026. Contractor shall pay $150 per day
for each day of delay beyond the completion date.

## 4. Permits and Inspections

Contractor shall obtain all required building permits at its own expense and
is responsible for scheduling and passing all inspections.

## 5. Change Orders

No extra or changed work shall be performed without a written change order
signed by both parties stating the additional price and any adjustment to the
completion date.

## 6. Lien Waivers

Contractor shall furnish unconditional lien waivers from itself and every
subcontractor and supplier with each payment request and before final payment.

## 7. Warranty

Contractor warrants all workmanship against defects for a period of two years
from substantial completion and shall correct defective work at no cost to
Owner.

## 8. Assignment

Contractor shall not assign this Contract or subcontract any portion of the
Work without Owner's prior written consent.

## 9. Cancellation

Owner may cancel this Contract within three business days of signing by
delivering written notice to Contractor, in accordance with the federal
cooling-off rule for home-solicitation sales. The deposit will be refunded in
full upon timely cancellation.

## 10. Disputes

The parties shall first attempt non-binding mediation. Any unresolved dispute
shall be heard in a court of competent jurisdiction in Owner's county.
