# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT — SAMPLE (manufacturer-unfavorable)

This Distribution Agreement is between Vertex Devices Inc.
("Manufacturer") and Global Retail Partners LLC ("Distributor").

## 1. Appointment — exclusive

Manufacturer appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor for
the Products in the Territory (North America). During the term,
Manufacturer shall not appoint any other distributor for the
Territory.

## 2. Trademarks

Distributor may use Manufacturer's trademarks, trade names, and logos
in all marketing, packaging, and promotional materials for the
Products, in any media, without restriction.

## 3. Local adaptations

All localizations, adaptations, and derivative works of the Products
created for the Territory shall be owned by Distributor, including
translated manuals, localized firmware, and territory-specific
packaging.

## 4. Post-term restriction

For two (2) years after expiration or termination, Manufacturer shall
not sell directly or appoint any other distributor in the Territory,
and shall not market competing products in the Territory.

## 5. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty
(30) days' written notice.

## 6. Pricing

Distributor shall receive Manufacturer's most favored pricing: if
Manufacturer offers lower prices or better terms to any other
customer or distributor, Distributor's prices shall automatically be
reduced to match.

## 7. Sales reporting

Distributor shall provide quarterly sales reports. Rebates and tier
discounts shall be calculated from these reports.

## 8. Distributor obligations

Distributor shall list the Products in its catalog and make them
available to its reseller network.
