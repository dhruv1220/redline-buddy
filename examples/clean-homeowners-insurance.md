# Homeowners Insurance Policy (HO-3) — Declarations and Exclusions (Policyholder-Friendly)

Policyholder: Jordan Lee
Property: 412 Seabreeze Lane, Galveston, TX
Coverage A (Dwelling): $800,000
Policy period: January 1, 2026 – January 1, 2027

## 1. Deductibles

Deductible: $1,000 per occurrence for all covered perils, including
windstorm and hurricane.

## 2. Loss Settlement

LOSS SETTLEMENT — Coverage A (Dwelling): Claims are settled on a
replacement cost basis once repairs are completed. No deduction is made
for depreciation.

## 3. Covered Perils

Where a covered peril is the predominant cause of a loss, coverage
applies even if an excluded peril also contributed to the loss.

Water backup endorsement included: up to $15,000 for loss from sewer and
sump-pump backup.

Mold and fungi remediation is covered up to $50,000 per occurrence.

## 4. Additional Living Expense / Loss of Use

Additional living expense: up to 30% of the Coverage A limit, payable for
up to 24 months while the dwelling is uninhabitable.

## 5. Settlement of Losses

Losses are settled in cash at the approved estimate; you choose the
contractor. The insurer may not require use of its own vendors.

## 6. Ordinance or Law

Ordinance or law coverage: included up to 25% of Coverage A for required
building-code upgrades after a covered loss.
