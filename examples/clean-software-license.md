# SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT (sample — balanced, licensee-fair)

This Software License Agreement is entered into by and between DataCorp Inc.
("Vendor") and Licensee.

## 1. License Grant

Vendor grants Licensee a perpetual, non-exclusive license to install and use
the Software on up to the number of seats purchased.

## 2. Audit Rights

Audits are limited to once per calendar year, on thirty days' written
notice, at Vendor's expense. Audit results are the confidential information
of Licensee.

## 3. Over-Deployment

License usage is measured annually. Over-deployment is cured prospectively
by purchasing additional seats at Licensee's then-current discounted rate,
with no retroactive charges.

## 4. Assignment

Licensee may assign this license to a successor in connection with a merger,
acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets without Vendor's
consent.

## 5. Support Lifecycle

Vendor shall support each major version of the Software for at least five
(5) years after its release.

## 6. Seat Commitments

Licensee may reduce the seat count at each anniversary of the effective
date, with fees adjusted pro rata. There is no floor on the number of seats
Licensee must maintain.

## 7. Support Fees

Support is optional and may be declined at renewal. If elected, support fee
increases are capped at five percent per year.

## 8. Refunds

If Vendor terminates without cause, prepaid fees are refunded pro rata for
the remainder of the paid period.

## 9. Source-Code Escrow

The Software source code is held in escrow with an independent escrow agent
and is released to Licensee if Vendor becomes insolvent or discontinues the
Software.
