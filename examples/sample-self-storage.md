# SELF-STORAGE RENTAL AGREEMENT — SAMPLE (renter-unfavorable)

SecureStore Facilities ("Operator") — Rental Agreement for Renter, Unit B-214.

## Rent

Monthly rent: $180. Operator may raise rent at any time at its sole
discretion. Rent increases may apply without prior notice.

## Late payment and lien

Rent is due on the 1st. A late fee of $25 applies after 5 days, plus
$5 per day late thereafter, plus a $75 lien administration fee. If rent
is unpaid for 45 days, Operator may enforce its lien and sell your
property and the contents of the unit at a lien sale or auction.
Operator may overlock the unit and deny gate access as soon as rent is
late. Property left unpaid for 60 days is deemed abandoned and may be
disposed of at Operator's discretion.

## Liability

Operator is not liable for theft, damage, mold, or loss of any kind,
and not responsible for damage from roof leaks or gate failures.
Renter waives all liability claims against Operator.

## Insurance

Renter must purchase insurance through the facility at $15/month.
Only the insurance we offer satisfies this requirement; outside
policies are not accepted.

## Access

Gate access is limited to office hours only (9am–5pm weekdays). There
is no 24-hour access.
