# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026,
by and between Apex Strategy Partners LLC ("Consultant") and the undersigned
client ("Client").

## 1. Services

Consultant shall provide digital transformation advisory services as described
in each Statement of Work ("SOW"). Consultant shall deliver the reports, models,
and documentation described in each SOW (the "Deliverables").

## 2. Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and shall automatically renew for
successive one-year terms.

## 3. Fees

Consultant's standard rate is $250 per hour. Consultant shall invoice monthly
and Client shall pay each invoice within 30 days (net 30). Consultant may
increase its rates annually effective each January 1.

## 4. Intellectual Property

All work product, deliverables, and materials created by Consultant in
connection with the Services shall remain the exclusive property of Consultant.
Client is granted a limited, non-transferable license to use the Deliverables
for its internal business purposes.

## 5. Confidentiality

Client acknowledges that Consultant's methodologies and materials are
confidential. Client shall not disclose Consultant's confidential information
to any third party during the Term and for three years thereafter.

## 6. Indemnification

Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Consultant from and against
any third-party claims arising from Client's use of the Deliverables.

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach upon 30 days'
written notice and opportunity to cure.

## 8. Non-Compete

During the Term, Client agrees to a non-compete and shall not engage any other
digital transformation consultancy for the services covered by this Agreement.

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.

## 10. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
