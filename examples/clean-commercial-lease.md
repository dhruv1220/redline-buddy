# COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is entered into as of June 1, 2026, between Parkside Retail LLC
("Landlord") and Brightline Coffee Co. ("Tenant") for Suite 120 at Parkside
Plaza.

## 1. Term

The initial term is five (5) years.

## 2. Rent and Operating Expenses

Base rent is $8,000 per month. Tenant shall pay its pro rata share of
operating expenses exceeding the base year of calendar year 2026. Controllable
CAM expenses are capped and shall not increase more than 5% per year; capital
expenditures and leasing commissions are excluded from CAM. Variable expenses
are grossed up to full occupancy. Tenant may audit Landlord's expense books
once per year within sixty (60) days of the reconciliation statement.

## 3. Permitted Use

Tenant may operate a coffee shop. No competing-use protection is granted;
Landlord may lease to other food and beverage tenants.

## 4. Assignment

Tenant may not assign this lease or sublet the premises without Landlord's
prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. Landlord may recapture
the space instead of consenting.

## 5. Casualty

If over 50% of the premises is destroyed or restoration would exceed twelve
(12) months, Landlord may terminate this lease on thirty (30) days' notice.

## 6. Holdover

Holdover rent is 150% of the then-current base rent, and Tenant is liable for
damages from delaying the succeeding tenant.

## 7. Relocation

Relocation is limited to once per term to comparable space, with all moving
and tenant-improvement costs paid by Landlord.

## 8. Guarantee

Tenant's principals execute a good-guy guarantee, liable only until Tenant
surrenders the premises in good condition with rent current.

## 9. Environmental

Tenant indemnifies Landlord for any hazardous-material contamination caused by
Tenant and represents it stores no hazardous materials beyond ordinary
business quantities.

## 10. ADA

Tenant is responsible for ADA compliance within the premises; Landlord is
responsible for ADA compliance in the common areas.

## 11. Insurance

Landlord and Tenant mutually waive subrogation for losses covered by their
respective property insurance policies.
