# BUSINESS TERM LOAN AGREEMENT (balanced, borrower side)

## 1. Loan Amount and Rate

$150,000 at a fixed interest rate of 7% per annum, 36 monthly installments.

## 2. Prepayment

Borrower may prepay in whole or in part at any time without penalty.

## 3. Default

Borrower has a 10-day grace period to cure any monetary default after written
notice.

## 4. Late Payments

Late payments incur a late fee of $50 per occurrence.

## 5. Collateral

Secured by the equipment listed in Exhibit A only.

## 6. Assignment

Lender may assign this loan only with Borrower's prior written consent.

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
