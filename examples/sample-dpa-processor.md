# Data Processing Agreement (Controller-Favoring)

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into by and between
Acme Corp ("Controller") and Vendor Inc. ("Processor").

## 1. Liability

Processor's liability for data protection failures, breaches of this DPA,
and privacy claims is uncapped and excluded from any liability cap in the
Master Agreement.

## 2. Audits

Controller has the right to audit Processor's facilities, systems, and
records at any time, without limitation on frequency, at Processor's
expense. Processor shall provide all assistance reasonably requested.

## 3. Deletion and Return

Upon termination, Processor shall delete all personal data within seven
(7) days and certify deletion in writing.

## 4. Sub-processors

Processor is liable for its sub-processors' failures as if they were its
own acts, without limitation.

## 5. Breach Notification

Processor shall notify Controller of any suspected personal data breach
within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of it.

## 6. Instructions

Processor shall follow all instructions issued by Controller from time to
time regarding the processing of personal data.

## 7. Data Subject Requests

Processor shall handle all data subject requests forwarded by Controller,
at its own cost and within Controller's timelines.

## 8. Suspension

Controller may suspend the services upon alleged breach of this DPA by
Processor, and may withhold payment during any suspected non-compliance.
