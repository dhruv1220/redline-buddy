# ADVISOR AGREEMENT (BALANCED)

This Advisor Agreement is entered into as of October 1, 2026, between
Nimbus Analytics, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Morgan ("Advisor").

## 1. Services

Advisor will provide product and go-to-market advice to the Company.

## 2. Compensation

Advisor receives 0.25% of the Company's fully diluted equity, vesting
monthly over two (2) years with no cliff.

## 3. Intellectual Property

Advisor assigns work-product IP created for the Company, retaining all
rights to Advisor's background IP and pre-existing materials.

## 4. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other's confidential information.

## 5. Expenses

Reasonable expenses will be reimbursed.

## 6. Term and Termination

The initial term is one (1) year. Either party may terminate this
agreement at any time on thirty (30) days' written notice.
