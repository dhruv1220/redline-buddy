# SAMPLE IP ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT — Novacore Systems (unfavorable to the Assignor)

## 1. Assignment
Assignor hereby assigns to Novacore Systems all inventions conceived at any
time, whether related to the Company's business or not, regardless of scope
of work, including all patents, copyrights, and trade secrets therein. This
assignment applies to work done on Assignor's own time with Assignor's own
equipment.

## 2. Disclosure
Assignor shall promptly disclose any invention conceived during the term of
this Agreement, and shall execute all documents the Company requests to
perfect its rights.

## 3. Post-Term Obligations
The assignment and disclosure obligations continue for twelve months following
termination of the relationship, and cover inventions conceived in that
trailer period.

## 4. Moral Rights
Assignor waives all moral rights and rights of attribution in the assigned
work worldwide, and consents to any alteration of the work.

## 5. Further Assurances
Assignor agrees to cooperate fully in securing the Company's rights, at
Assignor's expense, with no additional payment of any kind.
