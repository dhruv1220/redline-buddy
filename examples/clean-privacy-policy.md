# PRIVACY POLICY (sample — balanced, user-fair)

SnapShare Privacy Policy. Last updated: January 2026.

## 1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide and the usage data needed to operate
the service. We do not collect faceprints, voiceprints, or similar
body-measurement identifiers.

## 2. Data Sale and Sharing

Your personal information is never offered for purchase by third parties.
Advertising partners receive no personal data from us — only aggregated
statistics.

## 3. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time from settings,
and opt out of any sharing via the "Do Not Share" control. Deletion
completes within forty-five days.

## 4. Retention

We publish a clear retention schedule: account data is deleted within
ninety days of account closure, and logs within thirteen months.

## 5. Breach Notification

If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you
promptly after discovery.

## 6. Policy Changes

We email you thirty days before any material change to this policy takes
effect.
