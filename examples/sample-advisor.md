# ADVISOR AGREEMENT (ADVISOR-UNFAVORABLE)

This Advisor Agreement is entered into as of October 1, 2026, between
Nimbus Analytics, Inc. (the "Company") and Alex Morgan ("Advisor").

## 1. Services

Advisor will provide product and go-to-market advice to the Company.

## 2. Compensation

Advisor will be paid in stock at a later date.

## 3. Intellectual Property

Advisor hereby assigns all intellectual property created in connection
with the advisory services to the Company.

## 4. Non-Compete

Advisor shall not provide services to any competing business during the
term (the "Non-Compete").

## 5. Term

The initial term of this agreement is three (3) years.
