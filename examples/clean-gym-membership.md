# GYM MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT — CLEAN SAMPLE

IronWorks Fitness ("Club") — Membership Agreement for Member.

## Term and renewal

Month-to-month membership. This agreement does not auto-renew. Any new
term requires Member's affirmative written consent, given after at least
30 days' written notice of the renewal terms before the current term ends.

## Dues and fees

Monthly dues: $49. No initiation fee, no annual fee. Total first-year
cost: $588, fixed at signing.

## Cancellation

Member may cancel by any method used to enroll, including online,
email, or phone, effective within 15 days; no further dues accrue
after the effective date.

## Freeze policy

Member may freeze up to 3 months per year for medical reasons at no
charge; dues pause during the freeze.

## Personal training

Training sessions do not expire for 12 months; unused sessions are
refundable pro-rata.

## Liability

The liability waiver covers inherent exercise risks only and does not
waive claims from the Club's negligence.

## Closures

Dues are credited pro-rata for closures longer than 14 days.
