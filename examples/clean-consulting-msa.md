# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (balanced)

## 1. Services and Deliverables

Consultant shall provide the services in each SOW and deliver the reports,
models, and documentation described therein (the "Deliverables").

## 2. Intellectual Property

Consultant hereby assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in the work
product and deliverables, including all intellectual property rights therein.
Pre-existing IP, tools, and know-how owned by Consultant before the engagement
("Background IP") remain Consultant's property, licensed to Client as embedded
in the deliverables.

## 3. Fees and Payment

Consultant bills $200 per hour, invoices monthly, and Client pays undisputed
invoices net 30. Rate increases are capped at 5% per year with 60 days' notice.

## 4. Warranty and Acceptance

Consultant warrants the services will be performed professionally and the
deliverables will materially conform to the specifications. Client has 15 days
after delivery for acceptance testing of deliverables; silence is deemed
acceptance.

## 5. Liability and Indemnity

Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the twelve
months preceding the claim; this limitation of liability is mutual. Each party
agrees to mutual indemnification against third-party claims arising from the
indemnifying party's breach or negligence.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party's confidential information is protected; obligations are mutual and
survive two years after disclosure.

## 7. Term and Termination

This agreement will auto-renew for one-year terms unless either party gives 60
days' notice of non-renewal. Either party may terminate for convenience on 30
days' written notice, paying for work completed through the termination date.

## 8. Transition

On termination, Consultant provides transition assistance and knowledge
transfer for up to 30 days at the then-current rates.

## 9. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of California.
