# PURCHASE AGREEMENT (sample — seller-favoring)

Agreement for the purchase of 123 Main Street, Springfield.

## 1. Price and Deposit

Purchase price: $500,000. Buyer deposits $15,000 earnest money, which is
non-refundable under all circumstances.

## 2. Property Condition

The property is sold on an as-is basis. Buyer waives the right to have the
property examined by professionals.

## 3. Buyer's Obligations

If Buyer's loan application is denied, Buyer remains obligated to close at
the purchase price.

## 4. Seller Remedies

Seller may seek specific performance to compel closing, in addition to any
other remedies at law.

## 5. Costs

Buyer shall pay all closing costs and transfer taxes. Buyer assumes any
pending HOA special assessment.

## 6. Closing

Closing shall occur at a mutually convenient time and place.
