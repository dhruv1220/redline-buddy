# FRANCHISE AGREEMENT (sample — franchisor-favoring)

This Franchise Agreement is entered into by and between BurgerCo Franchising
LLC ("Franchisor") and Jane Smith ("Franchisee").

## 1. Territory

No territory rights are granted to Franchisee. Franchisor may open, operate,
or license competing outlets anywhere, including immediately adjacent to
Franchisee's location, without notice or compensation.

## 2. Fees

Franchisee shall pay a royalty of six percent (6%) of gross sales plus two
percent (2%) to the brand advertising fund. Franchisor may increase the
royalty fee at any time upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 3. Personal Guarantee

Each individual owner of Franchisee must execute and deliver a personal
guarantee of all of Franchisee's obligations under this agreement, jointly
and severally.

## 4. Non-Competition

Franchisee shall not compete with any quick-service restaurant business for
a period of two (2) years following expiration of this agreement, within a
fifty-mile radius of any BurgerCo outlet.

## 5. Supplies

Franchisee must purchase all food, packaging, and equipment exclusively
from Franchisor, at prices set by Franchisor in its sole discretion.

## 6. Transfer

Franchisee may not transfer this agreement or any interest in the
franchised business without Franchisor's prior written consent. Any
approved transfer requires payment of a $25,000 transfer fee.

## 7. Operations Manual

Franchisor may unilaterally amend the Operations Manual at any time.
Franchisee must comply with all amendments within fourteen (14) days of
notice.

## 8. Term

The initial term of this agreement is ten (10) years. Upon the end of the
term, Franchisee must immediately cease operating as a BurgerCo outlet and
de-identify the premises at Franchisee's expense.
