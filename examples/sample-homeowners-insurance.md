# Homeowners Insurance Policy (HO-3) — Declarations and Exclusions

Policyholder: Jordan Lee
Property: 412 Seabreeze Lane, Galveston, TX
Coverage A (Dwelling): $800,000
Policy period: January 1, 2026 – January 1, 2027

## 1. Deductibles

All-perils deductible: $2,500 per occurrence.
Windstorm/Hurricane Deductible: 5% of the Coverage A limit ($40,000 on this
$800,000 dwelling) per occurrence.

## 2. Loss Settlement

LOSS SETTLEMENT — Coverage A (Dwelling): Claims are settled on an actual
cash value basis (purchase price less depreciation for age and wear).

## 3. Exclusions

ANTI-CONCURRENT CAUSATION: We do not cover loss caused directly or
indirectly by any excluded peril, regardless of any other cause or event
contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss. If wind (a covered
peril) and flood (an excluded peril) combine to damage your home, no
coverage applies to the loss.

WATER BACKUP: Sewer backup and sump pump overflow are excluded perils.
We do not cover loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains.

MOLD: Mold, fungi, and rot remediation is limited to a $10,000 aggregate
sublimit per policy period.

## 4. Additional Living Expense / Loss of Use

Additional living expense while the dwelling is uninhabitable: limited to
20% of the Coverage A limit and a maximum of 12 months.

## 5. Insurer's Options After a Loss

INSURER'S OPTION: We may, at our option, repair or rebuild the damaged
property using contractors of our choice instead of paying a cash
settlement. The insurer's right to repair applies to all covered losses.

## 6. Code Compliance

Extra costs to bring repairs into compliance with current building codes
are the policyholder's responsibility.
