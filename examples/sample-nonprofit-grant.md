# SAMPLE GRANT AGREEMENT
(Deliberate red flags below — fixture for the nonprofit-grant playbook.)

1. Grant

The Foundation awards Grantee $100,000 for the after-school program.

2. Use of Funds

Grantee shall repay all grant funds upon the Foundation's demand. Indirect
costs are unallowable under this grant.

3. Reporting

Grantee shall submit monthly progress reports and monthly financial reports
to the Foundation.

4. Matching

Grantee shall provide 1:1 matching funds from other sources.

5. Intellectual Property

All work product shall be owned by the Foundation.

6. Termination

Funder may terminate this grant for convenience at any time.

7. Unspent Funds

Unspent funds shall be returned to Funder at the end of each year.

8. Personnel

This grant is contingent on the continued employment of the Executive
Director.
