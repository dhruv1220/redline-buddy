# HOUSEHOLD MOVING CONTRACT — SAMPLE (consumer-unfavorable)

This Moving Services Agreement is between QuickShift Relocations LLC
("Mover") and the Customer for a move from San Jose, CA to Austin, TX.

## 1. Estimate

This is a non-binding estimate of $3,100; final charges will be based
on actual weight and services performed on moving day.

## 2. Valuation

Mover's liability for loss or damage is limited to released value:
$0.60 per pound per article. This is the standard coverage included
with your move.

## 3. Deposit

A deposit of 50% of the estimated charges is due at booking and is
non-refundable. The balance is due in cash or cashier's check.

## 4. Payment and delivery

Mover may withhold delivery and refuse to unload until all charges,
including any additional fees assessed on moving day, are paid in
full.

## 5. Delivery schedule

Delivery will occur within a 5-to-21 business day spread after
pickup. Customer will be notified 24 hours before arrival.

## 6. Carrier

We are a licensed moving broker; your job will be arranged with an
independent motor carrier selected at our discretion.

## 7. Claims

All claims for loss or damage must be filed in writing within 30
days of delivery. Claims filed later will not be honored.

## 8. Additional charges

Additional fees may apply for stairs, long carries, fuel surcharges,
shuttle service, and packing materials used on moving day.
