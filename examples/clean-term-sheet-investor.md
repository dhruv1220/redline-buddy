# TERM SHEET — Seed Financing

This Term Sheet summarizes the principal terms of the seed financing of
Brightline Robotics, Inc. (the "Company").

## Securities

The Company will issue shares of Series Seed Preferred Stock to the Investor
at $2.00 per share, for an aggregate investment of $250,000. The shares carry
a 1x non-participating liquidation preference, senior to all Common Stock,
and are subject to broad-based weighted-average anti-dilution adjustment.

## Founder Vesting

All founder shares are subject to a vesting schedule: vesting over four years
with a one-year cliff. Unvested shares are subject to repurchase by the
Company at cost upon a founder's departure.

## Investor Rights

The Investor shall have pro-rata rights to purchase its pro-rata share of any
future equity financing. The Investor shall have information rights: the
Company will deliver quarterly financial statements and its annual budget,
and permit reasonable inspection of books and records.

The Investor shall have protective provisions: the following require Investor
consent — a sale of the Company, any charter amendment adverse to the Series
Seed shares, and the issuance of securities senior to the Series Seed shares.

## Board

The Investor may appoint one board observer with the right to attend all
Board meetings and receive all board materials.

## Drag-Along

All stockholders shall consent to a sale of the Company approved by the Board
and a majority of the Series Seed Preferred Stock (drag-along).
