# Master Subscription Agreement — balanced, provider-safe draft

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and
between CloudNine Inc. ("Provider") and Acme Corp ("Customer").

## Subscription

Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to
access the Service during the Term.

## Fees and Payment

Customer shall pay all invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.
Overdue amounts bear interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per
month. Provider may suspend the Service for non-payment continuing
fifteen (15) days after written notice.

Provider may increase subscription fees once per calendar year by up to
five percent (5%) upon sixty (60) days' written notice before renewal.

## Term and Termination

The initial term is one year, renewing for successive one-year terms.
Neither party may terminate except for the other party's material breach
uncured within thirty (30) days of written notice.

## Service Levels

Provider targets 99.9% monthly uptime. Service credits shall not exceed
ten percent (10%) of the subscription fees payable for the month in
which the downtime occurred.

## Intellectual Property

Provider retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Service,
including all intellectual property rights therein. Nothing in this
Agreement assigns any such rights to Customer.

## Limitation of Liability

Neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed the fees paid in the
twelve (12) months preceding the claim, excluding breaches of
confidentiality and infringement of intellectual property rights.

## Indemnification

Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Customer against
third-party claims alleging infringement of intellectual property rights
by the Service.

## General

This Agreement is the entire understanding between the parties.
