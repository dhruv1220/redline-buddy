# BUSINESS TERM LOAN AGREEMENT

This Loan Agreement is between QuickCap Funding LLC ("Lender") and the
undersigned borrower ("Borrower"), dated June 1, 2026.

## 1. Loan Amount and Rate

Lender agrees to lend Borrower $150,000. Interest accrues at a variable rate
tied to the prime rate. The interest rate is 9 percent per annum at closing.

## 2. Repayment

Borrower shall repay in 36 monthly installments. Any missed payment is an
immediate event of default. Late payments incur a late charge.

## 3. Prepayment

Prepayment of principal is subject to yield maintenance computed through the
maturity date.

## 4. Collateral

This loan is secured by a blanket lien on all assets of Borrower.

## 5. Guarantee

Borrower's obligations are personally guaranteed by the undersigned.

## 6. Confession of Judgment

Borrower hereby confesses judgment in favor of Lender for all amounts due
under this Agreement.

## 7. Assignment

Lender may assign this loan without notice to Borrower.

## 8. Disputes

All disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration.

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California.
