# CLEAN GRANT AGREEMENT
(Well-drafted — the nonprofit-grant playbook should fire nothing here.)

1. Grant

The Foundation awards Grantee $100,000 for the after-school program.

2. Use of Funds

Grantee may recover indirect costs at its federally negotiated indirect cost
rate (NICRA of 32%).

3. Reporting

Grantee shall submit annual narrative and financial reports within sixty (60)
days after each grant year.

4. Intellectual Property

Grantee retains ownership of all work product and grants Funder a perpetual
non-exclusive license to use it for charitable purposes.

5. Termination

Funder may terminate for convenience on ninety (90) days' prior written
notice. Grantee may retain funds for non-cancelable commitments made before
notice.

6. Unspent Funds

Grantee may carry over unspent funds to the next budget period with Funder's
prior written approval.

7. Personnel

If key staff depart, Grantee shall notify Funder within thirty (30) days and
propose a transition plan; funding continues during the transition.
