# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT (sample — balanced, distributor-fair)

This Distribution Agreement is entered into by and between SupplierCo Inc.
("Supplier") and Distributor.

## 1. Appointment

Supplier appoints Distributor as the exclusive distributor of the Products
in the Territory.

## 2. Prices

Wholesale prices are fixed for each calendar year and may increase by no
more than the CPI increase, on ninety (90) days' written notice.

## 3. Payment

After the first six (6) months, purchases are on net-thirty (30) day terms,
subject to Distributor remaining within its credit limit.

## 4. Pricing Freedom

Distributor sets its own resale and advertised prices, with no pricing
floors imposed by Supplier.

## 5. Non-Competition

During the term, Distributor shall not sell competing products. This
restriction does not survive the end of the agreement.

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate only for material breach not cured within ninety
(90) days' written notice.

## 7. Effect of Termination

Within ninety (90) days after termination, Supplier shall repurchase all
unsold inventory in resalable condition at Distributor's net purchase price.
On termination without Distributor's fault, Supplier shall also pay
goodwill compensation equal to one year's average gross margin.

## 8. Marketing

Supplier makes available a co-op marketing fund equal to two percent (2%)
of Distributor's net purchases, usable against approved local marketing.
