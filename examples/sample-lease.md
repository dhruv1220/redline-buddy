# RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is entered into as of April 1, 2026, between Harborview Properties LLC
("Landlord") and the undersigned tenant ("Tenant") for the unit at 88 Bay Street,
Apt 4B.

## 1. Term and Renewal

The initial term is twelve months. This lease shall automatically renew on a
month-to-month basis.

## 2. Rent

Monthly rent is $3,200, due on the first of each month. Landlord may increase
the rent each year.

## 3. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay 2 months' rent as a security deposit.

## 4. Repairs

Tenant shall be responsible for all repairs and maintenance of the premises.

## 5. Entry

Landlord may enter the premises at any time for any purpose.

## 6. Assignment

Tenant shall not sublet the premises or assign this lease.

## 7. Early Termination

Tenant may terminate this lease early upon ninety days' notice and payment of
three months' rent as an early termination fee.

## 8. Guarantee

All of Tenant's obligations under this lease are personally guaranteed by the
undersigned.

## 9. Disputes

Tenant shall pay all of Landlord's attorneys' fees arising from any dispute
under this lease.

## 10. Move-Out

Tenant shall return the premises in the same condition as received.
