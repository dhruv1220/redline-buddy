# Master Subscription Agreement — customer-favoring draft (provider review)

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and
between CloudNine Inc. ("Provider") and Acme Corp ("Customer").

## Subscription

Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive right to access the Service
during the Term. Fees are fixed for the initial three-year term.

## Payment

Customer shall pay all invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.

## Term and Termination

The initial term is three years. Customer may terminate this Agreement
for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice, with no further
obligation except payment for the period used.

## Service Levels

If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, Customer receives service credits
equal to 50% of the monthly fee for each hour of downtime, applied to
the next invoice.

## Intellectual Property

All intellectual property in the Service, including all customizations
and integrations built for Customer, is hereby assigned to Customer and
shall be deemed work for hire to the fullest extent of the law.

## Indemnification

Provider shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Customer against any
and all third-party claims, damages, costs, and expenses arising from
Customer's use of the Service.

## Audit

Customer may audit Provider's books and records twice per calendar year
to verify compliance with this Agreement.

## General

This Agreement is the entire understanding between the parties.
