# EQUIPMENT LEASE (sample — lessor-favoring)

This Equipment Lease is entered into by and between LeaseCo Inc. ("Lessor")
and Lessee for one (1) industrial printer.

## 1. Term and Rent

The lease runs for thirty-six (36) months at $2,000 per month, with no
right to end early. Lessee's payment obligations are absolute and
unconditional (hell or high water).

## 2. Renewal

This lease shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month
terms unless Lessee gives ninety (90) days' written notice of non-renewal.

## 3. Condition

The Equipment is accepted on an as-is basis. Lessor disclaims all
warranties, express or implied.

## 4. Insurance

Lessee shall maintain insurance on the Equipment for its full replacement
value at Lessee's expense.

## 5. Assignment

Lessee may not assign this lease or sublease the Equipment without Lessor's
prior written consent.

## 6. Late Payments

Late payments incur a five percent (5%) late fee plus collection costs.

## 7. End of Term

At term end, Lessee shall return the Equipment in good condition, ordinary
wear excepted.
