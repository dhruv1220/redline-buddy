# CONSULTING MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (balanced, vendor side)

## 1. Services and Fees

Consultant bills $250 per hour, invoiced monthly; Client pays net 30.
Overdue invoices accrue a 1.5% late fee per month.

## 2. Intellectual Property

Consultant hereby assigns all intellectual property in the work product to
Client. Pre-existing IP, tools, and know-how remain Consultant's property,
licensed as embedded in the deliverables.

## 3. Liability and Indemnity

Consultant's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid; this
limitation of liability is mutual. Each party agrees to mutual
indemnification against third-party claims.

## 4. Termination

Termination requires 30 days' written notice. On early termination without
cause, Client pays a kill fee of 50% of the remaining fees.

## 5. Personnel

The employee non-solicitation is mutual for twelve months.

## 6. Scope

Out-of-scope work needs a signed change order before it begins.

## 7. Cooperation

Client shall cooperate promptly; project timelines extend day-for-day for
Client-caused delays.

## 8. Insurance

Consultant maintains general liability ($1M/$2M) and professional liability
($1M) insurance.
