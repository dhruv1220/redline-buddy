# SAMPLE PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT, BALANCED — Harborline Ventures

## 1. Formation and Ownership
Alex Rivera and Jordan Kim form Harborline Ventures, a general partnership,
each holding a 50% interest. Ownership interests vest over four (4) years
with a one-year cliff; unvested interests are forfeited on departure.

## 2. Management and Deadlock
Day-to-day decisions are made by either Partner. Major Decisions require
unanimous consent. If the Partners deadlock on a Major Decision for 30 days,
the dispute goes to mediation, and if still unresolved, either Partner may
invoke the shotgun buy-sell procedure in Section 4.

## 3. Capital
Each Partner's Initial Contribution is $75,000. No Partner may be required to
fund the Partnership beyond the Initial Contribution without that Partner's
written consent.

## 4. Buyout on Exit
Upon a Partner's death, disability, divorce, or voluntary withdrawal, the
Partnership shall repurchase the exiting Partner's interest at fair market
value determined by an independent appraiser, payable over 24 months.

## 5. Intellectual Property
Each Partner assigns to the Partnership all right, title, and interest in the
Intellectual Property listed on Exhibit B that is used in the Partnership's
business. Pre-existing IP not listed on Exhibit B remains with the Partner.

## 6. Distributions
The Partnership shall make annual tax distributions sufficient to cover each
Partner's estimated tax liability on allocated income, within 90 days of
year end. Further distributions require majority approval.

## 7. Duties Between Partners
Each Partner retains the fiduciary duties of loyalty and care to the
Partnership and the other Partner, and shall disclose outside business
opportunities in the Partnership's field.

## 8. Post-Departure Restrictions
For twelve (12) months after departure, a Partner shall not solicit the
Partnership's customers listed on Exhibit C within the metropolitan areas
where the Partnership operates.
