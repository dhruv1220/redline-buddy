# EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is entered into as of October 1, 2026, between
Nimbus Analytics, Inc. (the "Company") and Jordan Lee ("Employee").

## 1. Position and Duties

Employee is hired as Senior Product Engineer. Employee shall devote full
business time to the Company and shall not engage in any other employment
or business activity during the term of employment.

## 2. Compensation

Base salary is $185,000 per year. Employee shall receive a $25,000 signing
bonus, which must be repaid in full if employment ends for any reason
within twelve (12) months of the start date.

## 3. Equity

Employee is granted 10,000 stock options vesting over four (4) years with
a one-year cliff. All unvested options are forfeited on termination of
employment for any reason.

## 4. Invention Assignment

Employee hereby assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in
all inventions, discoveries, and works of authorship, whether conceived
during or after employment and whether or not related to the Company's
business.

## 5. Confidentiality

Employee's obligations with respect to Confidential Information shall
survive in perpetuity.

## 6. Non-Compete

For twelve (12) months after termination, Employee shall not engage in
any business that competes with the Company (the "Non-Compete" covenant).

## 7. Non-Solicitation

For twelve (12) months after termination, Employee shall not solicit the
Company's employees or customers.

## 8. Garden Leave

The Company may place Employee on garden leave for up to six (6) months
following notice of termination.

## 9. Termination

The Company may terminate employment without cause upon ninety (90) days'
written notice.

## 10. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively by
binding arbitration in San Francisco, California. Employee waives any
right to a jury trial or to participate in a class action.
