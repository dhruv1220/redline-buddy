# Commercial Lease — landlord-favoring draft (tenant review)

This Commercial Lease ("Lease") is between Plaza Properties LLC
("Landlord") and Bean & Brew LLC ("Tenant") for 2,000 sq ft at
123 Main Street ("Premises").

## Rent

Base rent is $5,000 per month. This is a triple net lease: Tenant pays
all common area maintenance charges and operating expenses for the
Center, without offset.

## Security Deposit

Tenant shall deposit six months' rent as security deposit upon
execution.

## Term

The term is five years. Landlord may relocate Tenant's premises to
comparable space in the Center on ninety (90) days' notice.

## Repairs

Tenant is responsible for all structural repairs to the Premises,
including the roof, HVAC, and foundation.

## Guarantee

Tenant's principal shall execute a personal guaranty of all of
Tenant's obligations under this Lease.

## General

This Lease is the entire understanding between the parties.
