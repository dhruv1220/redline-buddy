# Commercial Lease — balanced, tenant-safe draft

This Commercial Lease ("Lease") is between Plaza Properties LLC
("Landlord") and Bean & Brew LLC ("Tenant") for 2,000 sq ft at
123 Main Street ("Premises").

## Rent

Base rent is $5,000 per month, plus Tenant's share of operating
expenses. Controllable CAM increases are capped at five percent
annually; capital expenditures are excluded.

## Security Deposit

Security deposit equals one month's Base Rent, returnable within
thirty (30) days after Tenant surrenders the Premises.

## Term and Renewal

The term is five years. Tenant has one option to renew for five (5)
years at fair market rent.

## Assignment

Tenant may assign or sublease with Landlord's consent, not to be
unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed.

## Exclusivity

Landlord grants Tenant exclusive use for coffee shop purposes in the
Center for the Term.

## Repairs

Landlord is responsible at its sole cost for the roof, foundation,
and structural elements of the Building.

## General

This Lease is the entire understanding between the parties.
