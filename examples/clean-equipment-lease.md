# EQUIPMENT LEASE (sample — balanced, lessee-fair)

This Equipment Lease is entered into by and between LeaseCo Inc. ("Lessor")
and Lessee for one (1) industrial printer.

## 1. Term and Rent

The lease runs for thirty-six (36) months at $2,000 per month. Rent abates
during any period the Equipment is materially unusable. Lessee may
terminate early on ninety days' notice by paying six months' rent.

## 2. Renewal

This lease does not auto-renew. Any renewal is month to month unless both
parties sign a new schedule, and Lessor shall remind Lessee 120 days before
term end.

## 3. Condition

Lessor warrants the Equipment is in good working condition at delivery.
Lessee may reject non-conforming Equipment within thirty (30) days.

## 4. Insurance

Lessor carries the master insurance policy and passes premiums through at
cost, not to exceed $1,200 per year.

## 5. Assignment

Lessee may assign this lease in connection with the sale of its business,
with Lessor's consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 6. Late Payments

Late payments bear interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month,
with no additional fees.

## 7. End of Term

Lessee has a $1.00 purchase option at term end, exercisable by written
notice thirty days before expiration.
