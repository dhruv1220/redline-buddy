# SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT — SAMPLE (contractor-unfavorable)

This Subcontract is between Apex General Contracting ("Contractor") and
Beacon Electrical Co. ("Subcontractor") for electrical work on the
Harborview Tower project (the "Project").

## 1. Payment — pay if paid

Subcontractor's payment is expressly conditioned upon Contractor's
receipt of payment from the Owner. This is a pay if paid arrangement:
if the Owner fails to pay Contractor for any reason, Contractor has no
obligation to pay Subcontractor. Subcontractor assumes the risk of
Owner non-payment.

## 2. Delays

Subcontractor's sole remedy for any delay, hindrance, or interference,
whether caused by Owner, Contractor, or others, shall be an extension
of time. Subcontractor waives any and all damages for delay, including
extended overhead and escalation.

## 3. Indemnification

Subcontractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Owner and
Contractor against any and all claims, damages, losses, and expenses
arising out of or related to the Work, regardless of fault.

## 4. Retainage

Contractor shall withhold retainage of 15% from each progress payment
until final acceptance of the entire Project.

## 5. Termination

Contractor may terminate this Subcontract for convenience upon seven
(7) days' written notice, or terminate without cause at any time.
Subcontractor's sole compensation upon such termination shall be
payment for Work completed as of the termination date, with no
additional compensation of any kind.

## 6. Liquidated damages

Time is of the essence. Subcontractor shall pay liquidated damages of
$2,500 per day for each calendar day of late completion of
Subcontractor's Work.

## 7. Lien waivers

Subcontractor shall execute and deliver an unconditional lien waiver
upon execution of this Subcontract, and further unconditional lien
waivers with each payment application, in advance of payment.

## 8. Prime contract

The prime contract between Owner and Contractor, including all
general conditions, is incorporated by reference herein. All
flow-down obligations of the prime contract apply to Subcontractor,
whether or not Subcontractor has reviewed the prime contract.
Subcontractor's obligations are subject to the provisions of the
prime contract.
