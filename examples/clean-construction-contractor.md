# SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT — SAMPLE (balanced)

This Subcontract is between Apex General Contracting ("Contractor") and
Beacon Electrical Co. ("Subcontractor") for electrical work on the
Harborview Tower project (the "Project").

## 1. Payment

Contractor shall pay Subcontractor within seven (7) days after
Contractor receives payment from the Owner for Subcontractor's Work.
Contractor's receipt of Owner payment is a matter of timing only and
is not a condition of Subcontractor's right to payment. Subcontractor
retains all lien rights available under applicable law.

## 2. Delays

For delays caused by Owner or Contractor, Subcontractor is entitled
to an equitable adjustment of both the schedule and the subcontract
price, including reasonable extended field overhead at the daily rate
in Exhibit C.

## 3. Indemnification

Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims to
the extent caused by the negligent acts or omissions of the
indemnifying party or its subcontractors.

## 4. Retainage

Retainage shall be 5% of each progress payment and shall be released
within thirty (30) days after substantial completion of
Subcontractor's Work.

## 5. Termination

Either party may terminate for convenience upon fourteen (14) days'
written notice. Upon such termination, Subcontractor shall be paid for
all work performed through the termination date, plus reasonable
demobilization and material restocking costs and a pro-rata profit on
work performed.

## 6. Liquidated damages

If Subcontractor fails to achieve substantial completion by the
scheduled date, liquidated damages of $500 per day shall apply, which
shall not exceed five percent (5%) of the subcontract price in the
aggregate.

## 7. Lien waivers

Subcontractor shall provide conditional lien waivers with each
payment application, each effective only upon Subcontractor's actual
receipt of the corresponding payment.

## 8. Prime contract

The portions of the prime contract between Owner and Contractor that
relate to Subcontractor's Work are attached as Exhibit A, and a copy
of the prime contract general conditions has been provided to
Subcontractor. Subcontractor assumes only the obligations expressly
listed in Exhibit B.
