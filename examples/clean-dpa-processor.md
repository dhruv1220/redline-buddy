# Data Processing Agreement (Balanced)

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into by and between
Acme Corp ("Controller") and Vendor Inc. ("Processor").

## 1. Liability

Each party's aggregate liability under this DPA shall not exceed the fees
paid in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim, including for data
incidents.

## 2. Audits

Audits are limited to once per twelve months on thirty (30) days' written
notice, at Controller's expense. Processor's current SOC 2 Type II report
satisfies this requirement.

## 3. Deletion and Return

Upon termination, Processor shall delete personal data within ninety (90)
days, excluding copies in backups retained until the normal rotation
cycle completes.

## 4. Sub-processors

Processor's liability for sub-processor failures is limited to its own
negligence in their selection or supervision, within the liability cap.

## 5. Breach Notification

Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay, and no later than
seventy-two (72) hours after confirming a personal data breach.

## 6. Instructions

Processor follows documented instructions within the agreed processing
scope. New purposes or data categories require a written change order,
and Processor may decline instructions that would violate applicable law.

## 7. Data Subject Requests

Processor assists with data subject requests; assistance beyond ten (10)
requests per month is billed at Processor's standard time-and-materials
rates.

## 8. Suspension

Suspension of services requires prior written notice, a thirty (30) day
cure period, and a confirmed material breach of this DPA.
