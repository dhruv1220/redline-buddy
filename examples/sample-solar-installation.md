# RESIDENTIAL SOLAR PPA — SAMPLE (homeowner-unfavorable)

This Solar Power Purchase Agreement is between BrightSun Energy LLC
("Installer") and the Homeowner for the property at 1234 Elm Street.

## 1. Term and rate

The initial term is twenty-five (25) years. Your PPA rate starts at
$0.16/kWh and will increase by 2.9% each year (the "Escalator") for
the duration of the term.

## 2. Ownership and title filing

Installer owns the System. Installer will file a UCC-1 financing
statement against the Property to protect its ownership interest in
the System, and Homeowner consents to such filing.

## 3. Roof work

Installer is not responsible for any roof damage, leaks, or water
intrusion arising from installation or the presence of the System.
Homeowner should consult a roofer before signing.

## 4. Payments during outages

Monthly payments continue without abatement during any period the
System is offline or removed, including during roof repairs,
equipment failure, or utility interconnection delays.

## 5. Removal and reinstallation

Homeowner shall pay all costs of System removal and reinstallation
($175 per panel) if roof work is required at any time during the term.

## 6. Sale of home

Any transfer of this Agreement is subject to Installer's credit
approval of the buyer and a $500 transfer fee. Installer may take up
to forty-five (45) days to process a transfer request.

## 7. Production estimates

Installer's production estimates are provided for illustration only
and create no obligation. Actual production may vary with weather,
shading, and equipment condition.

## 8. Entire agreement

Oral representations by salespersons are not binding on Installer;
only this written Agreement controls. Homeowner acknowledges that no
savings figures were promised.
