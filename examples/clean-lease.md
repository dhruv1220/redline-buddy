# RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (balanced)

## 1. Term and Renewal

Twelve-month initial term. This lease automatically renews month-to-month
unless either party gives 30 days' notice of non-renewal.

## 2. Rent

Monthly rent is $3,200, due on the first. Annual rent increases are capped at
3% with 60 days' notice.

## 3. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay 1 month's rent as a security deposit, refundable per state law.

## 4. Repairs

Landlord handles structural repairs; Tenant handles routine maintenance.

## 5. Entry

Landlord may enter with 24 hours' written notice except in emergencies.

## 6. Assignment

Tenant may sublet with Landlord's prior consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 7. Early Termination

Tenant may terminate early on 60 days' notice with a one-month termination fee.

## 8. Disputes

The prevailing party in any dispute recovers reasonable attorneys' fees.

## 9. Utilities

Tenant pays for all utilities serving the premises.

## 10. Move-Out

Tenant shall return the premises in the same condition as received, ordinary
wear and tear excepted.
