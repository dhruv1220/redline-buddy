# SAMPLE HEALTHCARE SERVICES AGREEMENT
(Deliberate red flags below — fixture for the healthcare-services playbook.)

1. Services

Vendor shall provide medical billing services to Provider. Vendor will have
access to patient records, including Protected Health Information, to perform
the billing work. Vendor may use Protected Health Information to improve its
own products and for its own business purposes.

2. Fees

Provider shall pay the fees in Exhibit A. Vendor may increase fees at any
time upon thirty days' notice.

3. Term

The initial term is one year. The Agreement renews automatically for
successive one-year terms.

4. Personnel

Provider shall not solicit Vendor's employees during the term of this
Agreement.
