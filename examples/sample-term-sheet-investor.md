# TERM SHEET — Seed Financing

This Term Sheet summarizes the principal terms of the seed financing of
Brightline Robotics, Inc. (the "Company").

## Securities

The Company will issue shares of Common Stock to the Investor at $2.00 per
share, for an aggregate investment of $250,000.

## Founder Shares

The founders' shares are fully owned outright as of the closing, with no
repurchase right held by the Company.

## Governance

The Board of Directors will consist of the two founders and the CEO. The
Company will operate in its ordinary course; major decisions rest with the
Board.

## Future Financings

The Company may raise additional capital on terms determined by the Board at
the time. Existing investors will be notified of new rounds as a courtesy.

## Sale of the Company

A sale of the Company requires approval of the Board of Directors and holders
of a majority of the Common Stock.

## Confidentiality

The Investor agrees to keep Company information confidential and not disclose
it to third parties.
