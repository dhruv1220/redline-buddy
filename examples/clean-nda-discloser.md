# NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (BALANCED, DISCLOSER-SAFE)

This Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into as of October 1, 2026,
between Nimbus Analytics, Inc. ("Discloser") and Vertex Partners LLC
("Recipient") to evaluate a potential business transaction.

## 1. Definition

Confidential Information means all non-public information disclosed by
Discloser, whether in writing, orally, visually, or electronically, and
whether or not marked.

## 2. Use Limitation

Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely to evaluate the
proposed transaction. No intellectual property rights are transferred.

## 3. Disclosure to Others

Recipient may disclose Confidential Information only to its
representatives with a need to know who are bound by written
confidentiality obligations.

## 4. Return or Destroy

Within thirty (30) days of termination or Discloser's request, Recipient
shall return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify
destruction in writing.

## 5. Survival

Recipient's confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years from
each disclosure, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 6. Remedies

Recipient acknowledges that breach may cause irreparable harm, and
Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other
remedies.

## 7. Compelled Disclosure

If Recipient is compelled by court order to disclose Confidential
Information, it shall give Discloser prompt written notice and cooperate
in seeking a protective order.

## 8. Term

This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years from
the Effective Date for new disclosures.

## 9. No Obligation

This Agreement creates no obligation for either party to enter into any
transaction.
