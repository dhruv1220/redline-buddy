# Data Processing Addendum — Clean (test fixture: fires nothing on dpa)

## 1. Subprocessors

Vendor shall notify Customer thirty (30) days before engaging a new
sub-processor. Customer may object to any sub-processor on reasonable grounds.

## 2. Return and Deletion

On termination, Vendor shall delete all personal data within thirty days and
certify deletion in writing.

## 3. Breach Notification

Vendor shall notify Customer of any personal data breach within 72 hours of
becoming aware of it.

## 4. Security Measures

Vendor implements technical and organizational security measures, including
encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, and annual penetration
testing.

## 5. Audits

Customer has the right to audit Vendor's compliance annually. Vendor maintains
a current SOC 2 Type II report available on request.

## 6. International Transfers

Cross-border transfers of personal data are governed by the EU Standard
Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
