# EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (BALANCED)

This Employment Agreement is entered into as of October 1, 2026, between
Nimbus Analytics, Inc. (the "Company") and Jordan Lee ("Employee").

## 1. Position and Duties

Employee is hired as Senior Product Engineer. Employee may engage in
outside employment and open-source work with the Company's prior written
consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, provided such activity does not
interfere with Employee's duties.

## 2. Compensation

Base salary is $185,000 per year. Employee receives a $25,000 signing
bonus, which is fully earned on the start date.

## 3. Equity

Employee is granted 10,000 stock options vesting over four (4) years with
a one-year cliff. Unvested equity is subject to double-trigger
acceleration if the Company is acquired and Employee is terminated without
cause within twelve (12) months of the acquisition.

## 4. Invention Assignment

Employee assigns inventions conceived using Company resources or relating
to the Company's business. Inventions made entirely on Employee's own time
with Employee's own equipment are excluded (see Cal. Labor Code
section 2870). Employee's prior inventions are listed in Exhibit A and are
excluded from assignment.

## 5. Confidentiality

Employee's confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after
separation and do not cover information that becomes publicly known or
was independently developed by Employee.

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate employment without cause on thirty (30) days'
written notice. If the Company terminates employment without cause,
Employee receives six (6) months of base salary as severance, paid as
salary continuation, plus Company-paid COBRA premiums for the same
period.

## 7. Dispute Resolution

Disputes under this Agreement shall be resolved in state or federal court
in San Francisco County, California.
