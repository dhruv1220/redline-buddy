# Venue Rental Agreement — Grand Ballroom Events

**Venue:** Grand Ballroom Events Center ("Venue")
**Organizer:** Brightline Conferences LLC ("Organizer")
**Event Date:** June 12, 2027

## 1. Rental

Venue rents the Grand Ballroom to Organizer for the event hours of 6:00 PM to 11:00 PM on the Event Date. Setup and teardown outside these hours are billed at the full hourly rate.

## 2. Fees

Rental fee: $25,000. Food and beverage minimum: $50,000 (500 covers at $100). No attrition allowance.

## 3. Cancellation

Cancellation more than six (6) months out forfeits 90% of all fees paid and contracted.

## 4. Vendors

Organizer must use Venue's exclusive in-house caterer and AV provider for all event services.

## 5. Damage

Organizer is liable for any and all damage to the Venue arising from the event, including restoration costs.

## 6. Relocation

Venue may relocate the event to a comparable room or reschedule the event date if required by Venue's bookings.

## 7. Sound

All amplified sound must end by 9:00 PM per the municipal noise curfew.

## 8. Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for failure to perform due to fire or flood.
