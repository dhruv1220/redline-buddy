# SAMPLE WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT — PixelForge Studio (unfavorable to the Client)

## 1. Scope and Ownership
PixelForge will design and build the Client's e-commerce website for $12,000.
PixelForge keeps full title to the website, its design, and all code —
the Client receives a non-exclusive license to use the live site.

## 2. Delivery
PixelForge will publish the finished website to the Client's domain. Only the
live website is delivered; no additional files are provided.

## 3. Payment
50% due on signing, 50% due on publication. All fees are non-refundable once
paid, and the final payment is due on publication with no testing period.

## 4. Quality
The website is provided as-is with no promises about quality. Post-launch
fixes and revisions are billed separately at PixelForge's standard rates.

## 5. Changes
Any change to the agreed scope — including text edits, image swaps, or layout
tweaks — is treated as a change request and billed hourly at $225/hour, with
no estimate or cap required before work begins.

## 6. Non-Payment
If any invoice is more than 14 days overdue, PixelForge may take down the
website and redirect the domain until all amounts are paid in full.
