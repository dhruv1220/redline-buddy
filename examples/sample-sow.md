# Statement of Work — Sample (test fixture)

This Statement of Work ("SOW") is entered into by Brightline Media LLC
("Client") and Alex Rivera ("Freelancer") effective October 1, 2026.

## 1. Services

Freelancer will redesign the Client's marketing website, including homepage,
pricing page, and blog templates, with two rounds of revisions. The project
will be delivered over six (6) weeks.

## 2. Compensation

Client will pay Freelancer a competitive project fee for the Services,
invoiced at mutually agreed milestones.

## 3. Term and Termination

Either party may terminate this SOW at any time for any reason on seven (7)
days' written notice.

## 4. Confidentiality

Freelancer shall keep all Client materials confidential during and after the
engagement.

## 5. Restrictive Covenants

For twelve (12) months after termination, Freelancer shall not perform
services for any competing business in the digital media industry.

## 6. Liability

Freelancer shall be liable for all damages, losses, and costs arising from
the Services, without limitation.
