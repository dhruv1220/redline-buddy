# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (DISCLOSER-UNFAVORABLE)

This Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into as of October 1, 2026,
between Nimbus Analytics, Inc. ("Discloser") and Vertex Partners LLC
("Recipient") in connection with discussions regarding a potential
business relationship.

## 1. Definition

Confidential Information means only information that is marked
'Confidential' in writing at the time of disclosure.

## 2. Residual Knowledge

Recipient may use Residual Knowledge retained in unaided memory without
restriction.

## 3. Disclosure to Others

Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its affiliates and
representatives.

## 4. Survival

Recipient's obligations survive for one (1) year from disclosure.

## 5. Remedies

Recipient's liability for breach shall be limited to direct damages.
