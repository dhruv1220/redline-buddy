# CLEAN HEALTHCARE SERVICES AGREEMENT
(Well-drafted — the healthcare-services playbook should fire nothing here.)

1. Services; Business Associate Agreement

Vendor shall provide medical billing services to Provider. The parties shall
execute the HIPAA Business Associate Agreement attached as Exhibit B, which is
incorporated by reference, before Vendor accesses any Protected Health
Information. Vendor shall use Protected Health Information solely to perform
the Services and shall access only the minimum necessary to do so.

2. Breach Notification

Vendor shall notify Provider of any suspected breach of unsecured Protected
Health Information without unreasonable delay and in no event later than ten
(10) days after discovery.

3. Fees

Provider shall pay the fees in Exhibit A. Vendor may increase fees by no more
than three percent (3%) in any twelve (12) month period.

4. Term; Renewal

The initial term is one year. The Agreement renews automatically for
successive one-year terms unless either party gives the other at least sixty
(60) days' written notice of non-renewal.

5. Service Levels

Vendor shall maintain 99.9% monthly uptime for the billing portal, excluding
scheduled maintenance, and shall issue service credits of 10% of the monthly
fee for each full percentage point below target.

6. Personnel

Neither party shall solicit the other party's employees during the term and
for twelve (12) months thereafter.

7. Exit

Within thirty (30) days after termination, Vendor shall return or securely
destroy all Protected Health Information and certify destruction in writing.
