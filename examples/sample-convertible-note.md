CONVERTIBLE PROMISSORY NOTE PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This Convertible Promissory Note Purchase Agreement (this "Agreement") is
entered into as of March 15, 2026, by and among Acme Robotics, Inc., a
Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and the purchasers listed on
Exhibit A (each, a "Noteholder").

1. Issuance of Notes. The Company shall issue convertible promissory
notes in the aggregate principal amount of $750,000 (each, a "Note").

2. Maturity. The Notes mature eighteen (18) months after the issuance
date (the "Maturity Date"). If a Qualified Financing has not occurred
on or before the Maturity Date, all outstanding principal and accrued
interest shall be due and payable in cash on the Maturity Date.

3. Interest. The Notes bear interest at a rate of 8% per annum,
compounding annually and accruing from the date of issuance. All
accrued but unpaid interest shall be added to principal.

4. Change of Control. Upon a Change of Control of the Company prior to
the Maturity Date or a Qualified Financing, each Noteholder shall be
entitled to receive, in cash, 2.5x the outstanding principal amount of
its Note plus all accrued interest, prior to any distribution to
holders of Common Stock.

5. Conversion; Shadow Series. Upon a Qualified Financing, the Notes
shall convert into shares of a "shadow series" of Series Seed Preferred
Stock, which shadow series shall carry a full liquidation preference
and participate with the Common Stock in any remaining distributions.

6. Conversion Price. The Notes shall convert into equity securities of
the Company issued in the Qualified Financing at a twenty percent
(20%) discount to the financing price, subject to a post-money
valuation cap of $8,000,000.

7. Qualified Financing. "Qualified Financing" means an equity financing
with aggregate gross proceeds of at least $10 million.

8. Noteholder Consent. Without the prior written consent of the
Noteholders holding a majority of the aggregate outstanding principal
amount of the Notes, the Company shall not (a) approve any annual
operating budget, (b) hire or terminate any executive officer, or
(c) incur indebtedness in excess of $50,000.

9. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
State of Delaware.
