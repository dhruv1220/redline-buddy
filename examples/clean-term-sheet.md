# Seed Term Sheet — Fair Terms Example (CLEAN)

This term sheet summarizes the proposed $1.5M seed financing of Example Corp (the "Company"). It is drafted to be founder-friendly and should trigger no red flags.

## Securities
Investors will purchase a SAFE. The SAFE converts at a 20% discount to the next equity financing, subject to a post-money **valuation cap** of $12M — whichever gives the Investor the lower price.

## Anti-Dilution
Anti-dilution protection is **broad-based weighted average**. Any harsher repricing formula is expressly excluded.

## Liquidation Preference
The Series Seed Preferred is **non-participating**: holders receive the greater of the 1x liquidation preference or the as-converted Common proceeds.

## Most Favored Nation
The Company grants MFN status with a **carve-out**: the MFN does not apply to strategic investor rounds, different classes of securities, or employee issuances.

## Pro Rata Rights
Each Major Investor may purchase up to its pro-rata share of future financings, based on as-converted ownership.

## Protective Provisions
Investor protective provisions are limited to fundamental corporate actions: charter amendments, creation of senior securities, indebtedness above $500K, and liquidation. Operating decisions remain with the Board.

## Founder Vesting
Founder shares vest monthly over four years with a one-year cliff, with **double-trigger acceleration**: on a change of control followed within 12 months by termination without cause, all unvested shares accelerate in full.

## Exclusivity
The Company agrees to exclusivity for a period of 30 days while diligence completes.

## Information Rights
Major Investors receive annual audited financials and quarterly unaudited statements.
