# Employment Agreement — employer-safe draft

This Employment Agreement is between Startup Inc. ("Company") and
Jordan Lee ("Employee"), effective January 5, 2026.

## Role and Compensation

Employee is hired as Senior Engineer at a base salary of $160,000 per
year. Employment is at-will and may be terminated by either party at
any time, with or without cause or notice.

## Invention Assignment

Employee hereby assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest
in all inventions, work product, and intellectual property created
within the scope of employment.

## Confidentiality

Employee shall not disclose the Company's confidential and
proprietary information during or after employment, and shall use it
solely for Company business.

## Non-Solicitation

For twelve (12) months after separation, Employee shall not solicit
the Company's employees or customers.

## Arbitration

Any dispute arising from employment shall be resolved by binding
arbitration under the applicable employment arbitration rules.

## Severance

Severance, if offered, is conditioned on Employee's execution of a
general release of claims in a form provided by the Company.

## General

This Agreement is the entire understanding between the parties.
