# FRANCHISE AGREEMENT (sample — balanced, franchisee-fair)

This Franchise Agreement is entered into by and between BurgerCo Franchising
LLC ("Franchisor") and Jane Smith ("Franchisee").

## 1. Territory

Franchisor grants Franchisee an exclusive territory covering the zip codes
listed in Exhibit A. Franchisor shall not open, operate, or license a
competing outlet inside the exclusive territory during the term, and
Franchisee has a right of first refusal on any new site within five miles.

## 2. Fees

Franchisee shall pay a royalty of five percent (5%) of gross sales plus one
and one-half percent (1.5%) to the brand advertising fund. These rates are
fixed for the term and may not be increased without the mutual written
agreement of both parties.

## 3. Guarantee

Franchisee's obligations are those of its corporate entity. No owner is
asked to backstop the agreement with personal assets, except for liability
arising from an owner's fraud or willful misconduct.

## 4. Non-Competition

During the term, Franchisee shall not compete with other outlets of the
System. This covenant does not survive the end of the agreement.

## 5. Supplies

Franchisee may purchase from any supplier meeting the quality and safety
standards published in the Operations Manual. Goods supplied by Franchisor
are priced at Franchisor's documented cost plus a disclosed markup not
exceeding ten percent.

## 6. Transfer

Franchisor's consent to a transfer of this agreement shall not be
unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Any charge for processing a
transfer is limited to Franchisor's documented administrative costs.

## 7. Operations Manual

Amendments to the Operations Manual take effect sixty (60) days after
written notice. Amendments that would increase Franchisee's operating costs
by more than five percent in any year require Franchisee's written consent.

## 8. Term and Continuation

The initial term is ten (10) years. Franchisee may renew for one
additional term of five (5) years on the same royalty economics, provided
Franchisee is not in material breach beyond applicable cure periods.
