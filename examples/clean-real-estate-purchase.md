# PURCHASE AGREEMENT (sample — balanced, buyer-fair)

Agreement for the purchase of 123 Main Street, Springfield.

## 1. Price and Deposit

Purchase price: $500,000. Earnest money of $15,000 is fully refundable
until all contingencies are removed.

## 2. Inspection

Buyer has fifteen days from acceptance for inspections and may cancel for
any inspection reason, with earnest money refunded.

## 3. Financing

This purchase is contingent on Buyer obtaining financing at the stated
terms within thirty days; if denied, Buyer may cancel with earnest money
refunded.

## 4. Condition and Warranties

Seller warrants the roof, foundation, plumbing, electrical, and HVAC are
in working order at closing; these warranties survive closing for one year.

## 5. Seller Remedies

Seller's exclusive remedy for Buyer's default is retention of the earnest
money as liquidated damages.

## 6. Costs

Each party pays its own closing costs and transfer taxes as customary in
the county. Seller pays or credits at closing any pending HOA charges
levied or noticed before closing.

## 7. Closing

The closing date is June 1, 2027, time being of the essence.
