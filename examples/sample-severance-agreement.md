# SEVERANCE AGREEMENT (sample — employer-favoring)

This Severance Agreement is entered into by and between TechCorp Inc.
("Company") and Employee.

## 1. Separation

Employee's employment ends effective March 15, 2026. Employee will receive
eight (8) weeks of severance pay, less applicable deductions.

## 2. Release

Employee provides a general release of all claims against the Company,
including unknown claims, in exchange for the severance pay.

## 3. Non-Disparagement

Employee agrees not to disparage the Company, its officers, or its products
in any public or private forum.

## 4. Cooperation

Employee shall cooperate with the Company in any litigation, investigation,
or proceeding involving the Company, on reasonable notice.

## 5. Non-Compete

Employee agrees that the non-compete in the employment agreement is extended
by an additional twelve (12) months from the separation date.

## 6. Rehire

Employee is ineligible for rehire by the Company or its affiliates.

## 7. Timing

Please sign and return this agreement within five (5) days of receipt.
