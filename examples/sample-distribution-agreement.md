# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT (sample — supplier-favoring)

This Distribution Agreement is entered into by and between SupplierCo Inc.
("Supplier") and Distributor.

## 1. Appointment

Supplier appoints Distributor as a non-exclusive distributor of the Products
in the Territory.

## 2. Prices

Supplier may increase wholesale prices at any time upon thirty (30) days'
written notice.

## 3. Payment

Prepayment is required for all orders before shipment. No credit terms are
offered.

## 4. MAP Policy

Distributor shall comply with Supplier's minimum advertised price policy as
published from time to time.

## 5. Non-Competition

Distributor shall not compete with the Products for a period of three (3)
years following termination of this agreement.

## 6. Termination

Supplier may terminate this agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days'
written notice.

## 7. Effect of Termination

Upon termination, Distributor shall cease all sales of the Products. All
inventory remaining with Distributor is Distributor's responsibility; no
returns are accepted.

## 8. Marketing

Distributor is responsible for all local marketing of the Products at its
own expense.
