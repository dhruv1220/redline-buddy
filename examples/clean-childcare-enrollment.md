# CHILDCARE ENROLLMENT AGREEMENT — CLEAN SAMPLE

Sunshine Daycare Center ("Center") — Enrollment Agreement for the Child.

## Enrollment

A registration fee of $100 is due at signing, credited in full toward
the first month's tuition. The enrollment deposit is refundable less a
$50 administrative fee if enrollment is cancelled before the start date.

## Tuition

Monthly tuition: $2,200. Families receive 10 vacation/absence days per
year credited against tuition; longer absences are billed at 50% of the
daily rate. Tuition is credited pro-rata for closures beyond the
published annual calendar.

## Withdrawal

Either party may terminate with 14 days' written notice; tuition is
owed only through the child's last day of attendance.

## Rate changes

Tuition increases are capped at 5% per 12-month period, require 60
days' written notice, and the family may end enrollment on 14 days'
notice without penalty.

## Late pick-up

Late pick-up is billed at $1 per minute after a 10-minute grace period,
capped at $30 per occurrence.

## Liability

The injury waiver covers inherent play risks only and does not waive
claims from the Center's negligence.

## Photos

Photos of the child are used for internal communications only; the
family may opt out of any marketing use at any time.
