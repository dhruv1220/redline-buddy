# CONSULTING MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is between Brightline Consulting LLC ("Consultant") and the
undersigned client ("Client"), effective May 1, 2026.

## 1. Services

Consultant shall provide data engineering services as described in each
Statement of Work, and shall deliver the reports, pipelines, and documentation
described therein.

## 2. Fees

Consultant bills $250 per hour, invoiced monthly; Client shall pay within 30
days (net 30).

## 3. Intellectual Property

Consultant hereby assigns to Client all intellectual property in the work
product and deliverables.

## 4. Indemnification

Consultant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client against all
third-party claims arising from the services.

## 5. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice.

## 6. Restrictive Covenants

Consultant agrees not to provide similar services to any competitor of Client
for twelve months after termination (non-compete). Consultant agrees to a
non-solicitation of Client's employees for twelve months.

## 7. Insurance

Consultant shall maintain all insurance reasonably required by Client.

## 8. Entire Agreement

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties.
