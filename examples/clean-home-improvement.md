# CLEAN HOME IMPROVEMENT CONTRACT
(Well-drafted — the home-improvement playbook should fire nothing here.)

1. Scope

Contractor, a licensed contractor (License #123456), shall remodel the
kitchen, including cabinets, countertops, and flooring, for a fixed price of
$45,000.

2. Payment

Homeowner shall pay a 10% refundable deposit upon signing. The balance is due
in milestones: 40% after demolition, 40% after cabinets are set, and 10% upon
substantial completion. With each payment application, Contractor shall
furnish unconditional lien waivers from Contractor and every subcontractor.

3. Schedule

Contractor shall obtain all required building permits before starting work.
Contractor shall achieve substantial completion by June 1, 2027, and shall
credit Homeowner $150 per day for each day of delay thereafter.

4. Changes

No extra work shall be performed until Homeowner signs a written change order
stating the price and any schedule impact.

5. Warranty and Insurance

Contractor warrants all workmanship against defects for one (1) year after
substantial completion. Contractor shall maintain commercial general liability
insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and name Homeowner as
additional insured.

6. Termination

Homeowner may terminate this Agreement on seven (7) days' written notice and
shall pay only for work completed through the termination date.
