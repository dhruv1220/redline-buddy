# Seed Term Sheet — Brightline AI, Inc.

This term sheet summarizes the proposed $1.5M seed financing of Brightline AI, Inc. (the "Company").

## Securities
Investors will purchase a SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity). The SAFE converts at a **20% discount** to the price per share of the Company's next equity financing. No cap is set — the conversion price floats entirely with the next round.

## Anti-Dilution
The SAFE includes **full ratchet** anti-dilution protection: if the Company issues shares below the conversion price before a priced round, the conversion price resets to the lowest issuance price.

## Liquidation Preference
Upon a priced round, the resulting Series Seed Preferred will be **participating preferred** — holders receive their 1x liquidation preference plus pro-rata participation with Common.

## Most Favored Nation
The Company grants each Investor **most favored nation** status: if the Company issues any future SAFE or convertible instrument on better terms, this SAFE is automatically amended to match. No exceptions are contemplated at this stage.

## Pro Rata Rights
Each Major Investor receives **super pro-rata** rights — the right to purchase up to 200% of its ownership percentage in the next equity financing.

## Protective Provisions
The following actions require Investor consent as **protective provisions**: charter amendments, senior securities issuance, the annual operating **budget**, and executive **hiring** decisions.

## Founder Vesting
Founder shares vest monthly over four (4) years with a one-year cliff. The term sheet is silent on what happens to unvested shares on acquisition or termination.

## Exclusivity
The Company agrees to **exclusivity** for a period of **60 days** (the "No-Shop Period") during which it will not solicit alternative financing proposals.

## Information Rights
Major Investors receive quarterly financial statements within 45 days of quarter end.
