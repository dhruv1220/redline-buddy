# Reseller Agreement — Balanced Channel Terms

**Vendor:** Acme Cloud, Inc. ("Vendor")
**Reseller:** Fabrikam Partners LLC ("Reseller")
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2026

## 1. Appointment

Vendor appoints Reseller as a non-exclusive reseller of the Products. Reseller may market and sell competing products at its discretion.

## 2. Pricing and Price Protection

Price increases are capped at five percent (5%) per twelve (12) months on ninety (90) days' notice; quoted deals keep the quoted price through acceptance.

## 3. Quotas and Marketing Support

Quotas apply only while Vendor funds the agreed MDF program and shares qualified leads; a missed quota triggers a ninety (90) day cure period, not termination.

## 4. Deal Registration

Registered deals are protected for one hundred eighty (180) days; Vendor shall not sell direct into a registered account below Reseller's quoted price.

## 5. Termination

Termination for convenience requires one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice, plus a ninety (90) day sell-through period for existing inventory and registered deals.

## 6. Amendments

Amendments require both parties' written agreement; program changes take effect on ninety (90) days' notice and do not apply to in-flight quotas or registered deals.

## 7. Indemnification

Vendor indemnifies Reseller against IP claims relating to the products as delivered; Reseller's indemnity covers only its own marketing materials and modifications.

## 8. Transition

For ninety (90) days after termination, Vendor shall provide transition assistance including support continuity and orderly customer handover.
